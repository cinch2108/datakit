      About the excel to csv

      Databases, CRMs, mailing tools and scripts almost always import CSV, not Excel. Saving as CSV from Excel works for one sheet at a time and often picks the wrong encoding or delimiter for your region. This converter opens XLSX, XLSM, XLSB, XLS and ODS workbooks directly in your browser, shows a preview of each sheet, and writes standards-compliant CSV with the delimiter, line endings and encoding marker you choose. Formatted dates and numbers are written the way they appear in the sheet, so what you see is what you get.

      How to use it

      1. Drop your workbook onto the box above.
      2. Choose a sheet from the list next to the file, or set Workbook sheets to All sheets.
      3. Pick the delimiter and line endings. Comma with CRLF suits most tools.
      4. Press Convert and download the CSV files, or all of them as a ZIP.

      Tips for the best results

      • Turn on Protect against formula injection before sharing a CSV that came from untrusted input. Cells starting with =, +, - or @ are prefixed so spreadsheet apps show them as text.
      • If you only need some sheets, Extract Excel Sheets lets you tick exactly which ones to save.
      • Merged cells are written once, in their top-left position, as in Excel's own CSV export.

      Settings explained

      How it works: spreadsheets (XLSX, XLSM, XLSB, XLS, ODS) are read and written by SheetJS, and CSV and TSV by Papa Parse, both running in a Web Worker so the page stays responsive. JSON, XML, YAML, SQL, Markdown and HTML are written by dedicated writers that escape every value for the target format. The text encoding is detected from the byte-order mark, falling back from UTF-8 to Windows-1252 for old Excel exports. Codes with leading zeros and IDs longer than 15 digits are always kept as text.

      Why use Sheetwise for this: most online excel to csv sites upload your spreadsheet to their servers, where it may be logged, kept or seen by staff, and many cap the rows or file size unless you pay. Here the file is read straight from your disk by JavaScript in this tab, processed in memory and handed back as a download. A strict Content Security Policy stops the page from contacting other servers, so the counter above always reads 0 bytes uploaded.

      Frequently asked questions

      Can I convert every sheet at once?

      Yes. Set Workbook sheets to All sheets, and you will get one CSV per sheet, named after the workbook and the sheet.

      Are formulas kept?

      CSV cannot store formulas, so each cell's last calculated value is written, as Excel does.

      Are my files uploaded anywhere?

      No. The file is read by your browser and processed in this tab with JavaScript. Nothing is sent to a server, which you can confirm with the 0 bytes uploaded counter or your browser's network panel.

      Does it open password-protected workbooks?

      No. Encrypted workbooks must be opened and saved without a password in Excel first.
