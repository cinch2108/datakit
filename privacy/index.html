Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

The short version

Your files are never uploaded. Every conversion, edit, merge, comparison, pivot and chart is processed by JavaScript running inside your own browser. We cannot see, store or share your data, because it never reaches us. Pasted text stays in the tab in the same way.

What we store on your device

  • localStorage, which is not a cookie, holds your theme, favorite tools, recent tool settings, a running total of bytes processed locally, and your ad-consent choice. You can clear it any time in your browser settings.
  • Your browser may cache the site's files so later visits load faster.

Clipboard

The Paste data button and Ctrl/⌘+V read your clipboard only when you use them. Copy buttons write to your clipboard only when you press them.

Analytics

We do not use analytics, fingerprinting or tracking pixels.

Advertising

To keep the tools free we may show ads from Google AdSense. If ads are enabled, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads and, if you consent, to personalize them. You can choose "Non-personalized only" in the consent banner, reopen it from the footer at any time, and manage Google ad settings at adssettings.google.com. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Children

The service is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

Questions? Email privacy@example.com (placeholder address; replace it before launch).
